# Synthetic ecological corridor planning

Implement `/app/workspace/project` so `python -m project.cli input.json output.json` works. This is an invented graph optimization puzzle, not ecological advice. `habitats` (2–7) each `{id,quality}` have unique nonempty ASCII IDs and nonnegative integer quality. `corridors` (0–10) each `{id,a,b,cost,capacity,open}` have unique ID, distinct valid habitat endpoints, positive integer build cost and capacity, and `open` is a list of season IDs in which the edge is passable; at most one corridor per unordered endpoint pair. `seasons` (1–3) each `{id,source,sink,demand}` have unique ID, distinct valid source/sink, positive integer demand. Input `budget` is nonnegative. Arrays may be unsorted.

Choose a subset of corridors with total build cost≤budget. In a season, use only chosen corridors listing that season's ID. An undirected corridor carries up to its capacity in **total net flow**, and the season is feasible when maximum source-to-sink flow ≥demand. Seasons are evaluated independently; capacity does not carry over across seasons. For each feasible subset, compute seasonal quality coverage: sum quality of every habitat reachable from that season's source by passable chosen corridors (regardless of capacity), including the source. Sum over seasons. Among subsets feasible in **all** seasons, minimize total build cost, then maximize summed quality coverage, then choose lexicographically smallest sorted corridor-ID list. Empty subset is allowed when no demand? Demand is always positive, so it may be infeasible.

Output `{status,selected,total_cost,coverage,season_flows}`. For feasible status, selected is sorted corridor IDs; total_cost and coverage integers; season_flows sorted `{season,max_flow}` rows. For infeasible status, selected/season_flows are empty and total_cost/coverage null. Compute the actual maximum flow, not just the threshold. Compact sorted-key JSON plus final newline, standard library only. Hidden tests cover seasonal closures, shared capacity bottlenecks, alternative investments, coverage tie-break and impossible budget. No Agent workflow is mandated.
