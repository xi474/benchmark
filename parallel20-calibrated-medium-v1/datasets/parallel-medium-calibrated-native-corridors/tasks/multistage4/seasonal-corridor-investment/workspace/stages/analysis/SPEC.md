# Shared seasonal corridor investment

This is a finite synthetic network-planning problem. habitats lists 2–12 distinct {id,quality} habitats with nonnegative integer quality. corridors lists at most 24 distinct {id,a,b,cost,capacity,open} undirected corridors. Endpoints are distinct valid habitat IDs and no two corridors share the same unordered endpoint pair. Build cost and capacity are strictly positive integers. open is a list of season IDs in which that corridor is passable. seasons lists 1–4 distinct {id,source,sink,demand} seasons with distinct valid source/sink and a positive integer demand. budget is a nonnegative integer. IDs are ordinary strings, references are valid, and no malformed-input or floating-point behavior is required. Input arrays can be unsorted.

Choose ONE subset of corridors to build with total build cost at most budget. The same investment is shared by every season. In each season use only chosen corridors listing that season in open. An undirected corridor carries up to its capacity in total NET flow: signed flow in either direction is bounded by capacity. At every other habitat flow is conserved. A season is feasible exactly when maximum source-to-sink flow is at least demand. Seasonal flows are evaluated separately, so seasons do not consume a persistent stock of capacity. Investment must be feasible in ALL seasons.

For a feasible investment, seasonal coverage is the sum of quality of all habitats reachable from that season's source using its passable chosen corridors, including the source. This reachability ignores capacity after passable edges are selected; coverage is not a sum of carried traffic. Sum the seasonal coverage values, counting a habitat once in each season in which it is reachable. First MINIMIZE total construction cost, then MAXIMIZE this summed coverage among lowest-cost feasible investments. Return ANY optimal corridor subset; no lexicographic ID tie-break is imposed.

Return {status,selected,total_cost,coverage,season_flows}. A feasible answer has status:'feasible', selected containing its distinct corridor IDs, total_cost and coverage integers, and season_flows containing one {season,max_flow} record per season. Report the ACTUAL maximum flow, even if it exceeds demand. Array and map order is irrelevant. Equivalent optimal investments are accepted after their legality, objective values, coverage and flow claims are independently recomputed. If no investment meets every seasonal demand within budget, return {status:'infeasible',selected:[],total_cost:null,coverage:null,season_flows:[]}.

Public example: S-X-T has two edges of cost 2, capacity 2, open in both summer and winter. A separate S-U-T summer-only path and S-V-T winter-only path each cost 3 and carry 2. Both seasons require 2 from S to T. Choosing the separately cheapest seasonal solutions costs 6; the globally best shared investment is S-X-T costing 4. Qualities S:1,X:4,T:2,U:0,V:0 give coverage 14 summed across the two seasons; each seasonal maximum flow is 2. If the budget is 3 no feasible investment exists. A capacity-one edge on the shared route cannot alone support demand 2, despite graph connectivity.
