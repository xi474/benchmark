# Port residency and battery-aware recovery

Input is a valid finite synthetic {periods,assets,grid_capacity,grid_ports}. periods1..6. There are1..5 assets, each {id,parent,repair_at,priority,battery_initial,battery_capacity,battery_discharge_limit,demand,solar,startup_cost,min_up,min_down}. IDs unique, parent null or another asset, a forest. repair_at0..periods; asset is grid-connected at t iff t>=repair_at for itself AND all ancestors. priority1..10,startup_cost1..5,min_up/min_down1..3,battery_capacity0..3,battery_initial0..capacity,discharge_limit0..3. demand/solar lists length periods with nonnegative integers0..3. grid_capacity/grid_ports are length-periods nonnegative integer lists, capacity0..5,ports0..asset_count. All data is known before planning, no real equipment or malformed-input handling.

Each asset starts port OFF, already rested long enough to turn ON at period0. At each period choose port_on boolean, grid_used integer>=0, battery_discharge integer>=0 for EVERY asset. An ON port requires its ancestor-connected line, consumes ONE shared grid port for that ENTIRE period even with grid_used0, and may remain ON with zero demand or zero grid capacity. OFF allows grid_used0 only. Turning OFF->ON incurs startup_cost once and requires ON for min_up consecutive periods including the starting period. Turning ON->OFF requires OFF for min_down consecutive periods including the stopping period. If a required residency extends beyond the finite horizon it may carry onward: no forced terminal OFF, cost or unknown future demand is imposed. Never violate a residency because ports/capacity change; plan earlier choices globally. Startup cost is charged for ON transitions even when grid_used0.

Energy order per asset per period: use min(solar,demand) directly as solar_used; solar surplus FIRST charges battery up to available space using battery BEFORE this period's discharge; remaining surplus is curtailed. Grid and battery discharge then serve remaining demand, may leave some unserved, but may not oversupply. battery_discharge<=battery_discharge_limit and <=battery_before+solar_charge. battery_final=battery_before+solar_charge-battery_discharge. Grid never charges batteries; discharge cannot free room retroactively for same-period solar. Solar/battery can operate while disconnected or port OFF. Shared sum(grid_used)<=grid_capacity[t], sum(port_on)<=grid_ports[t].

Across the FULL horizon, lexicographically maximize priority-weighted served units, then total served units, then MINIMIZE total startup cost. ANY globally optimal policy accepted; no ID, allocation, minimum ON-time, earlier-start or battery-final tie-break. Zero-delivery preactivation can be necessary for optimum: treating ON as equivalent to grid_used>0 loses legal useful policies.

Return exactly {score,periods,summary}. score={weighted_served,total_served,startup_cost}. periods in chronological order, each {t,assets,grid_unused,grid_active}; grid_active counts ON ports, grid_unused=grid_capacity[t]-sum(grid_used). Each assets list has EVERY asset once (any row order), each {id,connected,port_on,started,demand,solar,battery_before,solar_used,solar_charge,solar_curtailed,grid_used,battery_discharge,battery_final,served,unserved}; started true iff OFF->ON at that period, connected follows ancestor rule. summary sums demand,served,unserved,solar_used,solar_curtailed,grid_used, and adds weighted_served,battery_initial,battery_final,startup_cost; initial/final batteries sum over assets. The grader replays YOUR decisions and all energy/residency/resource ledgers, accepts any optimal policy.

Public case:3 periods,grid_capacity[0,1,1],grid_ports[1,1,1]. A priority10,demand[0,0,1],min_up1,startup_cost1; B priority9,demand[0,1,0],min_up2,startup_cost2; both min_down1,no battery/solar,parent null,repair_at0. Turn B ON in period0 with grid0; keep B ON and grid1 in period1; turn B OFF and A ON with grid1 in period2. score {weighted_served:19,total_served:2,startup_cost:3}. If B starts only at its nonzero demand in period1 it remains ON in period2 and blocks A. Public full input and expected ledger are provided.
